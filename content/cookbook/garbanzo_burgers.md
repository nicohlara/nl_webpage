Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup dry garbanzos, precooked (or 1 can)
- 1 onion
- 1 8-oz package mushrooms
- 1/3 cup dry brown rice, cooked
- Spices to taste (e.g. cumin, cayenne, garam masala)
- Olive oil

Instructions:
- Cook garbanzos and brown rice as needed.
- Sauté minced onions and mushrooms until translucent.
- Mash all ingredients together until mixed but not puréed.
- Form into patties ~ 1/4-1/3 cup.
- Bake on 425 F 15 minutes, flipping halfway through.
