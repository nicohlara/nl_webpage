Ingredients:
- 1 onion
- 8 oz. mushrooms
- white wine
- herbs/spices (sage, oregano, paprika)
- Butter (vegan or real)
- Olive oil

Instructions:
- Fry onion in olive oil on low until translucent but not caramelized or burned.
- Add rough-chopped mushrooms, a splash of white wine, dash of salt, and paprika and herbs (if dried). Cook until liquid has evaporated.
- Add butter and fresh herbs and cook, stirring frequently, until butter is melted and incorporated.
- Blend everything together.
- Cook until thickened and let cool.
